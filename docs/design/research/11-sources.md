# 11 · Sources

Research carried out 17 Oct 2026. Most web sources are practitioner blogs and vendor guides, so they were treated as opinion and cross-checked. Standards (WCAG, NHS, Carbon) take precedence.

## Skills and tools used

- **UI-UX Pro Max** design-intelligence datasets: style, colour, typography, landing, UX guideline and chart searches. Its Neumorphism and cyan suggestions were rejected (accessibility risk, brand mismatch).
- **Dataviz skill:** chart method, and the palette validator (`validate_palette.js`) used to validate the emerald chart palette in light and dark mode.
- **WCAG contrast** computed for every colour pair in [02](02-color-system.md).
