## Platform

web

## Users

- People in Rwanda looking for care for themselves or family: often on a phone, sometimes a
  shared or low-end Android device on a slow connection, reading in Kinyarwanda, English or
  French. They want to know where to go, whether it is open, how long the wait is, and whether
  their insurance (Mutuelle, RAMA, MMI, private) is accepted.
- Someone in an emergency, who needs 912 and the nearest hospital, not a product.
- Health facilities deciding whether to join, and officials evaluating the service _(inferred
  from the brief: "if this system would be used by someone like the president")_.

## Product Purpose

MediLink is a national platform that lets patients find facilities, services and doctors, see
live waiting times, and join a queue or book; facilities run the visit from check-in to pharmacy.
Success on the public site: a person finds the right place and acts within a minute, and trusts
what they see.

## Positioning

Live queue and opening information from the facilities themselves, across the whole country, in
three languages, with health data stored in Rwanda.

## Operating Context

- Facilities are public health posts, health centres, district, provincial and referral hospitals,
  private clinics, polyclinics and specialist clinics; each has a Ministry of Health facility code
  (FOSA ID), a place (province to village) and opening hours.
- Emergencies: 912 (SAMU) is free, 24 hours.
- Patients may have no account; search needs none.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public pages: landing, find care, facilities, doctors, services, facility and doctor pages,
  check symptoms, AI assistant (test), emergency, how it works, about, for facilities, verify a
  document, help, contact, privacy, terms, accessibility.
- Light and dark themes; three languages; 44 px minimum targets on the public site.
- No prices are shown (decision 2026-11-02).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: MediLink Rwanda; existing logo and the forest-green brand colour.
- Owner's binding brief (2026-11-04): must not look AI-generated; held to the standard of large
  technology companies (Microsoft, Google), which owner named for their stable, well-made
  backgrounds and cards; **health facilities must not look like products in cards.**
- An Imigongo border motif appears once, on the footer's top edge.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: the facility, service and doctor data the API serves; live queue counts.
- Absent: real facility photographs (images in development data are illustrative), testimonials,
  usage numbers. None may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Facts before persuasion: the page answers where, when, how long and whether it is covered.
2. A public service, not a shop: places are listed and described, never merchandised.
3. Emergency is always one step away and visually distinct from everything else.
4. Calm under stress: plain words, clear hierarchy, nothing that moves without reason.
5. Works on a small phone on a slow network, in every language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA; 44 px targets; text resizes; reduced motion respected; content readable in
Kinyarwanda, whose words run long.
